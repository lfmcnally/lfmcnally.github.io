For the classroom

Lesson plan builder

Fill in your objectives, the stages of the lesson and how long each one takes, and print a tidy lesson plan or download it as a Word document. Timings add up as you go, and saved plans stay in this browser. Choose the standard layout, or an evidence-informed one with sections for inclusive teaching, learning intentions and formative assessment. It works for any subject.

Format

Anything you had in the matching standard boxes is copied across the first time you switch, and the Students boxes join the tasks. Hover over or tap the ? beside a box to see what goes in it.

Your own sections

Add anything else the plan needs, such as homework or resources. Each section is printed after the lesson’s tasks.

Layout

Anything left empty is left off the plan. Tick “space to write in” to keep every section, which makes a blank template to print and fill in by hand. Click any heading in the preview to rename it.

Saved plans

Saved plans are stored in this browser only, so they stay on this computer and are lost if you clear your browsing data. Save to a file to keep a copy elsewhere or share it with a colleague.
