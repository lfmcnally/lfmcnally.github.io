Slides or a web page
Open your presentation: a PDF saved from PowerPoint gives an exact copy of your slides, and the .pptx itself gives a quick version. Pictures of slides work too, and nothing is uploaded. Or show a web page, such as a Classicalia page, on this side instead.
To make a PDF in PowerPoint: File → Save As → PDF (or Export → PDF on a Mac). A link from PowerPoint Online (File → Share → Embed) or Google Slides keeps your animations, and you move through it with its own arrows. Some websites will not let themselves be shown inside another page; for those, the page offers to open them in a new tab.

Keys and tips

→, Space, Page DownNext slide (a presentation clicker works too)
←, Page UpPrevious slide
1 – 4Side by side, one above the other, small window, one side only
XSwap the two sides
DPen on and off; Esc puts it away
PFreeze the visualiser
FFull screen
Ctrl + ZUndo the last line you drew

Drag the bar between the two sides to change their sizes. In the small-window layout, drag the window by its top edge, and pull any edge or corner to make it bigger or smaller. Writing on a slide stays with that slide as you move back and forth.

The slides side can show a web page instead: press Open, then Web page or link. Use the + and − on its bar to make the page bigger for the back of the room. While you are clicking about inside a web page, the keys above go to that page; click anywhere outside it to have them back.

Your layout is remembered, and so is the last presentation you opened on this computer.
