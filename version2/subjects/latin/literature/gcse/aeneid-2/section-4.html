40There, first, before everyone, with a great crowd accompanying,
41Laocoon runs down blazing from the height of the citadel
42and from a distance [shouted], ‘O pitiable citizens, what insanity is this?

44dōnă cărērĕ dŏlīs Dănăūm? sīc nōtŭs Ŭlīxĕs?
43Do you believe the enemy have been taken away? Do you think any
44gift from the Greeks is without deception? Is Ulysses thus known?
45aūt hōc īnclūsī līgno͜ ōccūltāntŭr Ăchīvī,
46aūt haēc īn nōstrōs făbrĭcāta͜ ēst māchĭnă mūrōs
47īnspēctūră dŏmōs vēntūrăquĕ dēsŭpĕr ūrbī,
48aūt ălĭquīs lătĕt ērrŏr; ĕquō nē crēdĭtĕ, Teūcrī.
45Either the Greeks are hidden here, shut in the wood,
46or this machine was made [to be used] against our walls
47to look into our homes and to come down on our city from above
48or some other trick lies hidden; do not trust the horse, Trojans.
49quīcquĭd ĭd ēst, tĭmĕō Dănăōs ēt dōnă fĕrēntīs.”
49Whatever it is, I fear the Greeks even bearing gifts’
