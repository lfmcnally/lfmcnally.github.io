25
We reasoned they had left and had sought Mycenae with the wind.
26
Therefore all of Troy released itself from the long sorrow;
27
The gates are opened; It is pleasing to go and to see the Doric camp
28
and the deserted places and abandoned shore.
29
Here band of Dolopians, here fierce Achilles was encamped;
30
Here was the place for the fleets, here they used to fight in battle array.
31
Some are astounded by the fatal gift for virgin Minerva,
32
and wonder at the size of the horse; and Thymoetes first
33
encourages it to be led within the walls and to be placed in the citadel,
34
either through deceit or the fates of Troy were already leaning thus.
35
But Capys, and those of better sense of mind,
36
suspected traps of the Greeks and order that they throw the gifts
37
headlong into the sea or they burn them, having kindled them with flames,
38
or to bore into the hollows of the belly and try for hiding places.
39
The uncertain people are split into contrary desires.
