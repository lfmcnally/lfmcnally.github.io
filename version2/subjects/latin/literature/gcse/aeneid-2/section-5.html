52cōntōrsīt: stĕtĭt īllă trĕmēns, ŭtĕrōquĕ rĕcūssō
53īnsŏnŭērĕ căvaē gĕmĭtūmquĕ dĕdērĕ căvērnaē.
50Having spoken thus, he hurled a huge spear with his powerful strength
51into the side and into the belly of the beast curved with timbers:
52it stood there quivering, and by the reverberation of the womb
53The hollows sounded and the caverns gave a groan.
54ēt, sĭ fătā dēūm, sī mēns nōn laēvă fŭīssĕt,
55īmpŭlĕrāt fērro͜ Ārgŏlĭcās foēdārĕ lătēbrās,
56Troīăquĕ, nūnc stārēt, Prĭămīque͜ ārx āltă, mănērēs.
54and, if divine fate, if our minds had not been perverse,
55He would have driven us to spoil the Greek hiding places with our swords,
56and Troy would now stand, and you, the high citadel of Priam would remain.
