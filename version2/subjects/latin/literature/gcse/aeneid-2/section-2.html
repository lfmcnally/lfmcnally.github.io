40
There, first, before everyone, with a great crowd accompanying,
41
Laocoon runs down blazing from the height of the citadel
42
and from a distance [shouted], ‘O pitiable citizens, what insanity is this?
43
Do you believe the enemy have been taken away? Do you think any
44
gift from the Greeks is without deception? Is Ulysses thus known?
45
Either the Greeks are hidden here, shut in the wood,
46
or this machine was made [to be used] against our walls
47
to look into our homes and to come down on our city from above
48
or some other trick lies hidden; do not trust the horse, Trojans.
49
Whatever it is, I fear the Greeks even bearing gifts’
50
Having spoken thus, he hurled a huge spear with his powerful strength
51
into the side and into the belly of the beast curved with timbers:
52
it stood there quivering, and by the reverberation of the womb
53
The hollows sounded and the caverns gave a groan.
54
and, if divine fate, if our minds had not been perverse,
55
He would have driven us to spoil the Greek hiding places with our swords,
56
and Troy would now stand, and you, the high citadel of Priam would remain.
57
But lo! Meanwhile some shepherds, with much noise, were dragging
58
a young man with his hands shackled behind his back in front of the king of the
59
Trojans, who offered himself of his own accord as a stranger to those approaching,
60
that he might construe this very thing and open Troy to the Greeks.
61
relying on his spirit he was prepared for either outcome
62
either to weave his deceit or to die a certain death.
63
in their eagerness to see the man the Trojans
64
rushed from everywhere to vie with each other in mocking the captive
65
Hear now of the Greek treachery and from this one crime
66
learn of all.
67
For all to see in the centre of the crowd, he stood defenceless
68
and his eyes looked around the Trojan ranks,
69
‘Alas!’, he said ‘is there now any land or any sea that is able
70
to receive me? Or is there now nothing left to me in this misery
71
who has no place among the Greeks, and even moreso
72
the Trojans are baying for my blood?
73
With a groan our minds are changed and all our passions
74
were checked. We pressed him to tell us his nationality
75
and what he was to do; to recount why he trusted to be captured.
