14dūctōrēs Dānaūm, tōt iām lābēntĭbŭs ānnīs,
15īnstār mōntĭs ĕquūm dīvīnā Pāllădĭs ārtē
16aēdĭfĭcānt, sēctāque͜ īntēxūnt ābiĕtĕ cōstās:
17vōtūm prō rĕdĭtū sĭmŭlānt; eā fāmă văgātūr.
14the leaders of the Danaans, when so many years had already slipped by,
15built a horse like a mountain with the divine skill of Athena
16and they wove the ribs from cut down fir:
17They pretend it is a votive for their return; that is the rumour that is spread.
18hūc dēlēctā vīrūm sōrtĭtĭ cōrpŏră fīrtĭm
19īnclūdūnt caēcō lătĕrī, pĕnĭtūsquĕ căvērnās
20īngēntīs ŭtĕrūmque͜ ārmātō mīlĭtĕ cōmplēnt.
18In this they secretly enclose a body of men chosen by lot
19within its dark sides, and they fill the cavernous insides
20and its huge belly with armed soldiers.
21ēst īn cōnspēctū Tĕnĕdōs, nōtīssĭmă fāmă
22īnsŭlă, dīvĕs ŏpūm, Prīāmĭ dŭm rēgnă mănēbānt,
23nūnc tāntūm sĭnŭs ēt stătĭō mălĕ fīdă cărīnĭs:
24hūc sē prōvēctī dēsērto͜ īn lītŏrĕ cōndūnt.
21There is, within sight of Tenedos, the island famous
22in stories, rich in wealth, while the kingdoms of Priam were standing,
23now it is only a bay and a not-so-good standing for boats:
24Here they settled themsleves, having been carried onto the deserted shore.
