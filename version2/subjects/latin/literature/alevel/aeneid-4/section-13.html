So he spoke, and tore his flashing sword from its scabbard, and with the drawn blade struck at the mooring ropes. The same urgency seized everyone at once; they snatch things up, they rush; they have already abandoned the shore, the sea vanishes beneath the ships, and, straining together, they churn the water to foam and sweep across the blue.
