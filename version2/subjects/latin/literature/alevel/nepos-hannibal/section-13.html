classe paucis diebus erant decreturi. superabatur navium multitudine: dolo erat
Within a few days the fleets were due to settle matters. Hannibal was outnumbered in ships, and [would have to fight|10,2.1]
pugnandum, cum par non esset armis. imperavit quam plurimas venenatas serpentes vivas
[by trickery|1.9], since he could not match his enemy in strength. He ordered as many live venomous snakes as possible
colligi easque in vasa fictilia conici.
to be gathered and packed into earthenware pots.
harum cum effecisset magnam multitudinem, die ipso, quo facturus erat navale proelium,
Once he had collected a great number of these, on the very day set for the naval battle,
classiarios convocat hisque praecepit, omnes ut in unam Eumenis regis concurrant navem;
he called his marines together and gave the order: all of them were to converge on the one ship carrying King Eumenes,
a ceteris tantum satis habeant se defendere. id illos facile serpentium multitudine
while against everyone else they need only hold their own defensively — the sheer number of snakes, he told them,
consecuturos.
would take care of [the rest|6.8].
rex autem in qua nave veheretur, ut scirent, se facturum. quem si aut cepissent aut
He would make sure they knew which ship the king himself was sailing on: whoever captured or
interfecissent, magno iis pollicetur praemio fore.
killed [him|8.11], he promised, would receive a rich reward.
