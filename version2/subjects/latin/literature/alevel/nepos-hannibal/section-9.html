Grammarpari diligentia ... ac 'with equal care as'; fuerat is pluperfect of esse but is better translated 'shown', to balance with se ... praebuit.
Chiasmuspari diligentia se Hannibal praebuit, ac fuerat in bello — the word order here (adjective-noun-verb ... verb-noun) mirrors itself around the middle, an a-b-b-a pattern called chiasmus, which nicely reflects the idea of Hannibal's peacetime and wartime selves being mirror images of each other.

Vocabularyvectigalibus a vectigal was an import-export tax, from veho, vehere, vexi, vectus ('to carry').
Anaphoraquae Romanis ex foedere penderetur ... quae in aerario reponeretur — both clauses start with quae, matching in structure. It lays out Hannibal's financial success as a neat, two-part achievement: he covers what Carthage owes, and then some.
ContextUnder the treaty, Carthage owed Rome 10,000 talents over 50 years, working out at 200 a year. Just like his father before him, Hannibal was really preparing for the next war as soon as the last one ended — which is exactly why he had opponents at home, who eventually reported him to Rome.

Grammarpublicarunt and iudicarunt are syncopated forms of publicaverunt and iudicaverunt.
