at Hannibal anno tertio postquam domo profugerat, L. Cornelio Q. Minucio consulibus,
In the third year after fleeing his home, in the consulship of Lucius Cornelius and Quintus Minucius,
cum quinque navibus Africam accessit in finibus Cyrenaeorum si forte Carthaginienses ad
[Hannibal|1.2] reached Africa with five ships, in the territory of Cyrene, to see whether [he might draw|3.5,3.6] the Carthaginians into
bellum Antiochi spe fiduciaque inducere posset, cui iam persuaserat ut cum exercitibus in
Antiochus's war by holding out the promise of his support — Antiochus he had already persuaded to [invade|13,4.2]
Italiam proficisceretur. huc Magonem fratrem excivit.
Italy [with|3.11] [his forces|3.12]. From there he sent for his brother Mago.
id ubi Poeni rescierunt, Magonem eadem, qua fratrem, absentem affecerunt poena. illi
As soon as the Carthaginians learned of this, they punished Mago in his absence exactly as they had punished Hannibal.
desperatis rebus cum solvissent naves ac vela ventis dedissent, Hannibal ad Antiochum
Their situation now hopeless, [the two brothers|5.12] set sail, and Hannibal [reached|7.1]
pervenit. de Magonis interitu duplex memoria prodita est. namque alii naufragio, alii a
[Antiochus|6.11,6.12]. Two different accounts survive of Mago's death: some [record|8.5,8.6] that [he|8.4] [died|8.3] in a shipwreck, others that [he was killed|8.3,8.4]
servulis ipsius interfectum eum scriptum reliquerunt.
[by|7.13] his own slaves.
Antiochus autem, si tam in gerendo bello consiliis eius parere voluisset, quam in suscipiendo
As for Antiochus, if he had been as willing to follow Hannibal's advice in fighting the war as [he had been|10.1] in undertaking it,
instituerat, propius Tiberi quam in Thermopylis de summa imperii dimicasset. quem etsi
he would have fought for supreme power nearer the Tiber than at Thermopylae. [Yet|11.5] even when
multa stulte conari videbat, tamen nulla deseruit in re.
he could see [the king|10.11] pursuing many foolish schemes, Hannibal never once abandoned him.
praefuit paucis navibus, quas ex Syria iussus erat in Asiam ducere, hisque adversus
He was given command of a small squadron, which he had been ordered to lead from Syria to Asia, and with these ships [he fought|13,13.6]
Rhodiorum classem in Pamphylio mari conflixit. quo cum multitudine adversariorum sui
the fleet of Rhodes in the Pamphylian Sea. Though his side [was outnumbered|9,14.1] by the enemy overall,
superarentur, ipse, quo cornu rem gessit, fuit superior.
on the wing where he himself fought, he came out on top.
