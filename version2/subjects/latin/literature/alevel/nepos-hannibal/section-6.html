hac pugna pugnata Romam profectus est nullo resistente. in propinquis urbi montibus
After fighting this battle he set out for Rome, unopposed. [He waited|2.1,2.2] for [some|2.4] [days|2.6] in the hills near the city,
moratus est. cum aliquot ibi dies castra habuisset et Capuam reverteretur, Q. Fabius
and, having camped there, was on his way back to Capua when Quintus Fabius
Maximus, dictator Romanus, in agro Falerno ei se obiecit.
Maximus, the Roman dictator, blocked his path in Falernian territory.
hic clausus locorum angustiis noctu sine ullo detrimento exercitus se expedivit Fabioque,
Trapped by the narrowness of the terrain, Hannibal got his army out that night without a single loss, and [tricked|5.3,5.4] Fabius
callidissimo imperatori, dedit verba. namque obducta nocte sarmenta in cornibus
— for all his skill as a commander. Under cover of darkness he had bundles of twigs [tied|6.2] to the horns
iuvencorum deligata incendit eiusque generis multitudinem magnam dispalatam immisit.
of young cattle and set alight, then sent a great many of these animals scattering across the hillside.
quo repentino visu obiecto tantum terrorem iniecit exercitui Romanorum, ut egredi extra
The sudden, strange sight terrified the Roman army so completely that [not one man|8.2] [dared|8.3,8.4] go beyond
vallum nemo sit ausus.
the rampart.
hanc post rem gestam non ita multis diebus M. Minucium Rufum, magistrum equitum pari
Not many days after this, [he lured|10.5] Marcus Minucius Rufus — the Master of Horse, holding [power|10.3] equal
ac dictatorem imperio, dolo productum in proelium fugavit. Ti. Sempronium Gracchum,
to the dictator's — into battle by a trick, and routed him. [He destroyed|11.9] Tiberius Gracchus,
iterum consulem, in Lucanis absens in insidias inductum sustulit. M. Claudium Marcellum,
consul for the second time, by luring him into an ambush in Lucania while Hannibal himself was elsewhere, and [killed|12.7] Marcus Claudius Marcellus,
quinquies consulem, apud Venusiam pari modo interfecit.
five times consul, near Venusia in much the same way.
longum est omnia enumerare proelia. quare hoc unum satis erit dictum, ex quo intellegi
It would take too long to list every battle. This one fact will be enough [to show|12,13,14,14.1]
possit, quantus ille fuerit: quamdiu in Italia fuit, nemo ei in acie restitit, nemo adversus eum
how great a man he was: as long as he remained in Italy, no one stood against him in open battle, and no one dared
post Cannensem pugnam in campo castra posuit.
pitch camp [against him|14.15,14.16] in the open field after Cannae.
