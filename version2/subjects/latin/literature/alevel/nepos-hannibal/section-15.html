Styleconsilio ... superavit the chapter's final word on the battle is that Hannibal won by consilium, 'strategy' or 'cleverness', not by force. It's a fitting close, since this whole episode — like the money hidden in statues in Crete — is really there to prove Hannibal's brains, not his brawn.
1

2

4–5

5–6

8–9

10–11
Thus Hannibal overcame the forces of Pergamum through strategy; and that was not the only time, but often on other occasions too he drove back his opponents on land with equal cleverness.
