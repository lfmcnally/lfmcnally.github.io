conflixerat apud Rhodanum cum P. Cornelio Scipione consule eumque pepulerat. cum hoc
He had already fought the consul Publius Cornelius Scipio at the Rhone and driven him back; with this
eodem Clastidi apud Padum decernit sauciumque inde ac fugatum dimittit.
same man he fought again at Clastidium on the Po, and sent him off wounded and routed.
tertio idem Scipio cum collega Tiberio Longo apud Trebiam adversus eum venit. cum his
A third time this same Scipio, now with his colleague Tiberius Longus, met him at the Trebia;
manum conseruit, utrosque profligavit. inde per Ligures Appenninum transiit, petens
Hannibal [engaged|1,2,3.13] [them both|3.14,3] and crushed them. From there he crossed the Apennines through the territory of the Ligurians,
Etruriam.
[making for|4.10] Etruria.
hoc itinere adeo gravi morbo afficitur oculorum, ut postea numquam dextro aeque bene
On this march he was struck by such a severe eye infection that afterwards he never [had|7.1,7.2]
usus sit. qua valetudine cum etiam tum premeretur lecticaque ferretur, C. Flaminium
the [full|6.12,6.13] use of his [right eye|6.11] again. While still suffering from this and being carried in a litter, [he ambushed|8.6,8.7] and [destroyed|8.8] the [consul|8.1] Gaius Flaminius
consulem apud Trasumenum cum exercitu insidiis circumventum occidit neque multo post
and his army at Lake Trasimene, and not long after that
C. Centenium praetorem cum delecta manu saltus occupantem.
the praetor Gaius Centenius, along with his picked troops holding the mountain passes.
hinc in Apuliam pervenit. ibi obviam ei venerunt duo consules, C. Terentius et L. Aemilius.
From here he reached Apulia, where the two consuls, Gaius Terentius and Lucius Aemilius, came out to meet him.
utriusque exercitus uno proelio fugavit, Paulum consulem occidit et aliquot praeterea
In a single battle he put both their armies to flight, killed the consul Paulus, and several [ex-consuls|12.1]
consulares, in his Cn. Servilium Geminum, qui superiore anno fuerat consul.
[besides|11.11], among them Gnaeus Servilius Geminus, who had been consul the year before.
