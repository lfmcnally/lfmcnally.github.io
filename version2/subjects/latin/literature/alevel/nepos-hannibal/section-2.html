nam ut omittam Philippum, quem absens hostem reddidit Romanis,
For, to pass over Philip, whom Hannibal turned into Rome's enemy without ever meeting him,
omnium iis temporibus potentissimus rex Antiochus fuit.
King Antiochus was, in those days, the most powerful ruler of all.
hunc tanta cupiditate incendit bellandi,
Hannibal inflamed him with so great a desire for war
ut usque a rubro mari arma conatus sit inferre Italiae.
that he attempted to bring his forces against Italy all the way from the Red Sea.
ad quem cum legati venissent Romani,
When Roman envoys came to Antiochus's court
qui de eius voluntate explorarent darentque operam consiliis clandestinis
to sound out his intentions and to work secretly behind the scenes
ut Hannibalem in suspicionem regi adducerent,
to make the king suspicious of Hannibal,
tamquam ab ipsis corruptus alia atque antea sentiret,
as though he had been bribed by them and now thought differently than before —
neque id frustra fecissent idque Hannibal comperisset
and their scheme did not fail, and Hannibal found out about it,
seque ab interioribus consiliis segregari vidisset,
and realised he was being shut out of the king's inner counsels —
tempore dato adiit ad regem,
he went to the king as soon as the chance arose,
eique cum multa de fide sua et odio in Romanos commemorasset,
and after reminding him at length of his loyalty and his hatred of Rome,
hoc adiunxit: ' pater meus' inquit
he added this. 'My father,' he said,
Hamilcar puerulo me, utpote non amplius VIIII novem) annos nato,
'Hamilcar, when I was a small boy — no more than nine years old —
in Hispaniam imperator proficiscens Carthagine,
as he was setting out from Carthage for Spain as commander-in-chief,
Iovi optimo maximo hostias immolavit.'
offered sacrifice to Jupiter, greatest and best of the gods.'
