Hannibal, Hamilcaris filius, Carthaginiensis.
Hannibal, son of Hamilcar, a Carthaginian.
si verum est, quod nemo dubitat,
If it is true — and no one doubts it —
ut populus Romanus omnes gentes virtute superarit,
that the Roman people surpassed every nation in courage,
non est infitiandum Hannibalem tanto praestitisse ceteros imperatores prudentia,
then it cannot be denied that Hannibal outstripped every other commander in skill by just as great a margin
quanto populus Romanus antecedat fortitudine cunctas nationes.
as the Roman people exceed all nations in bravery.
nam quotienscumque cum eo congressus est in Italia,
For as often as anyone engaged with him in Italy,
semper discessit superior.
he always came away the victor.
quod nisi domi civium suorum invidia debilitatus esset,
And had he not been weakened at home by the jealousy of his own countrymen,
Romanos videtur superare potuisse.
he seems to have been capable of defeating Rome itself.
sed multorum obtrectatio devicit unius virtutem.
But the resentment of the many overpowered the courage of one man.
hic autem velut hereditate relictum odium paternum erga Romanos
Yet he [kept alive|12.2] the hatred of his father towards the Romans, handed down to him almost as an inheritance,
sic conservavit, ut prius animam quam id deposuerit,
so faithfully that he would have given up his life before setting it aside —
qui quidem, cum patria pulsus esset et alienarum opum indigeret,
indeed, even once he had been driven from his homeland and depended on the resources of others,
numquam destiterit animo bellare cum Romanis.
he never once stopped waging war on Rome, at least in spirit.
