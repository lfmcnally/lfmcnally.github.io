train [the king|2.14] against Rome.

Seeing that Prusias lacked the resources to stand on his own, Hannibal won over other kings to his side
adiungebat bellicosas nationes. dissidebat ab eo Pergamenus rex Eumenes, Romanis
and drew warlike nations into alliance with him. Eumenes, king of Pergamum, [a close ally|6.1] of Rome,
amicissimus, bellumque inter eos gerebatur et mari et terra;
[was at odds|5.4] [with|5.5] [Prusias|5.6], and war was being fought between them on both land and sea;

but on both fronts Eumenes had the advantage, thanks to his alliance with Rome. This only made [Hannibal|8.2]
eum Hannibal opprimi; quem si removisset, faciliora sibi cetera fore arbitrabatur. ad hunc
[more|7.10] [determined|7.11] to see him crushed — if he could remove Eumenes, he reasoned, everything else would come more easily.
