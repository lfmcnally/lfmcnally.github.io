About this course
  • BoardOCR H408/11
  • LevelA-level Classical Civilisation
  • Lessons14 written of 43 planned

The compulsory epic, taught across a year in six topics — one per half-term, following the poem in order. Prescribed books: 1, 5–13, 16–19 and 21–23. Every book has an annotated reading workbook to download; the unprescribed books are marked as bridges — useful background for 30-mark essays, never examined in context questions. Each topic carries a lead theme taught explicitly, while other threads are seeded and revisited as the poem unfolds. The full scheme of work sets out the 37-lesson spine.
