Notes on the text 1
88
Ἀριστόδικος, -ου, ὁAristodicus
Aristodicus
A citizen of Cyme, known only from this story; Herodotus names his father as Heraclides. His name is made from ἄριστος, "best", and δίκη, "justice".
Questions to think about · 2
  1. Why do you think Aristodicus blames the envoys rather than the oracle itself?
  2. What does Herodotus tell us about Aristodicus that makes it believable that one man could stop a whole city?
