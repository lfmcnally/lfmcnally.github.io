4 · Course page: Overview

/latin/gcse/

The centre of the site. One page per course for visitors, students and teachers alike; the five tabs hold everything that used to be spread over the hub, the set-text folders, the tester and the dashboard.

These are layouts, not visual designs: the real pages keep Classicalia's navy, cream, Lora and DM Sans. What changes is the structure (one header, course pages with tabs, one lesson and one set-text template), the weight of inner-page headers, and the honesty of what is shown as available. The decisions each layout depends on are listed in section 10 of SITE_REDESIGN.md.
