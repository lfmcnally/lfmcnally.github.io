For teachers

Teaching Classics? Run your classes on it.

Whether you are a specialist or covering Latin for the first time, the site gives you something to set tonight and a way to see who did it.

  • Create a class and add students by their sharing code. Co-teachers welcome.
  • Set homework on a chapter range or a topic, with a target: every word right five times, the last two typed.
  • Start a live Do Now and watch the leaderboard fill in.
  • See each student's confidence map, the words the class is stuck on, and who has gone quiet.
  • Weekly tests marked with help from AI against your own mark scheme, with your override on every mark.

About Classicalia

Written by a teacher, free for everyone

Classicalia is written by Lawrence McNally, Deputy Head of Classics at Clifton College, Bristol, and winner of the Classical Association's Outstanding New Teacher Award in 2021. It began as a set of Odyssey booklets for his own students and has grown into a site used in schools across the country.

It has no adverts, no paywalls and no subscriptions. The next things to be written are the rest of The Homeric World, Topics 2 to 6 of the Odyssey, and exam practice with mark schemes for GCSE Latin.
